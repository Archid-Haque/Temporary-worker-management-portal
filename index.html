Work records

Each record keeps the calculation and its history together.

Required flow

  1. Record task, date and agreed rate.
  2. Employer records attendance.
  3. Worker confirms or disputes it.
  4. Add overtime, extra work, advances and partial payments.
  5. Show earned − advances − completed payments = pending.

Important rule

Confirmed records are locked against silent changes. Both sides see the same history. This is a record-keeping tool, not a payment service or legal dispute authority.
